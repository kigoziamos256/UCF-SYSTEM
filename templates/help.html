Getting Started

Welcome to University Community Fellowship's online system. This platform lets you:

  • Mark your attendance at services
  • View church events and announcements
  • Track your duties
  • Update your profile picture
  • Watch live streams on YouTube
Tip: You can install this as an app on your phone — see the Install as App section below.

Signing Up (New Members)

1
Go to Register from the top menu (or tap Don't have an account? Register on the login page).
2
Enter your username, email, and a password (minimum 8 characters).
3
Choose your department (e.g., Worship, Youth, Choir).
4
Optionally add a phone number and profile picture.
5
Tap Register. You'll be logged in automatically.

Signing In with Google

Skip the form entirely — use your Google account:

1
On the login page, click Sign in with Google.
2
Choose your Google account.
3
You'll be asked to pick a department and optionally upload a picture. Your Google photo will be used if you don't upload one.
Note: We never see your Google password. We only get your name, email, and photo.

Your Profile

Access it via the dropdown at the top right → My Profile.

You can:

  • Update your profile picture (camera icon over the photo)
  • See your upcoming events and pending duties
  • View your stats (events attended, duties completed)
iPhone users: You can take a photo or choose from your library. HEIC photos are converted automatically.

Marking Your Attendance

There are two ways to mark attendance:

Option A — Scan the QR code at the entrance
1
Open your phone's camera (no special app needed).
2
Point it at the UCF poster at the church entrance.
3
Tap the link that appears — it opens the UCF registration page.
4
Fill in your name + email → tap Record My Attendance. Done!
Option B — Show your personal QR code

Get your personal QR from Services → My Attendance QR in the menu. Show it to a leader at the door and they'll scan it.

If you're logged in already, the system knows you. If not, your email links you to your account automatically.

Services (Sunday & Mid-Week)

From the Services dropdown, you can view:

  • Sunday Service — First service (8:30–10 AM) and Second service (11 AM–1 PM), with flyers and YouTube live links
  • Mid-Week Service — Wednesday (6–8 PM)
  • My Attendance QR — your personal code
Tap the red Watch Live on YouTube button to open the live stream in your browser.

Events

All church events are listed in the Calendar section.

  • Tap any event to see full details, cover image, and location
  • Press Join Event to RSVP — your name will be listed as an attendee
  • You can unjoin later by pressing Cancel Attendance

Duties

If a leader assigns you a duty (e.g., ushering, cleaning), you'll see it on your dashboard under My Pending Duties.

  • Each duty has a title, description, and due date
  • Once you finish it, tap the duty → mark as completed
  • You'll receive a notification when a new duty is assigned

Notifications

The bell icon at the top right shows new alerts about:

  • New events you might want to join
  • Duties assigned to you
  • New announcements

Tap any notification to mark it as read. A red badge shows unread count.

Install as an App

On Android (Chrome)
1
Open the site in Chrome.
2
Tap the ⋮ menu → Install app or Add to Home screen.
On iPhone (Safari)
1
Open the site in Safari.
2
Tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen → Add.
On Desktop (Chrome/Edge)
1
Look for the install icon in the address bar (a small monitor with a down arrow).
2
Click it → Install.
The app will open full-screen with its own icon — just like a real app store app, but no download needed.

For Leaders & Admins

Posting Flyers

Admin menu → Upload Service Flyer. Add the flyer image and the YouTube live stream link.

Printing Entrance QR Codes

Admin menu → Entrance QR Codes. Download the PNG and print it on A4 for the church entrance.

Attendance Reports

Admin menu → Attendance Report. Filter by date/service, then click Export CSV to open in Excel.

Managing Finance

Admin menu → Finance Department to record tithes, offerings, expenses, budgets, and requisitions.

Troubleshooting

"Session Expired" or "Forbidden (403)"

This usually means cookies were blocked. Try:

  • Reload the page (Ctrl + Shift + R on desktop)
  • Open the site in a normal browser tab, not WhatsApp's built-in browser
  • Clear cookies for the site and log in again
"Site can't be reached" on my phone

Check your internet connection. If the issue persists, wait 30 seconds and reload — Render's free tier sleeps after inactivity.

Photo upload fails on iPhone

Make sure the photo is under 15 MB. HEIC files are converted automatically — no action needed.

Can't log in with Google

Make sure you're using the same Google account you signed up with. If it's your first time, use Sign in with Google on the login page.

Still stuck? Contact your church admin or WhatsApp the UCF media team.
